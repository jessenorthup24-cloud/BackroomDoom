# NOCLIPPED

Bodycam backrooms survival horror shooter for mobile browsers.

**Play:** open the GitHub Pages link for this repo on your phone, then use *Add to Home Screen* (Safari) or *Install app* (Chrome) to install it. It plays fullscreen in landscape and works offline after the first load.

Controls (touch): swipe left side to move, right side to aim; it fires automatically when the crosshair is on a target. Double-tap the right side to aim down sights. Tap your health to use a medkit, tap your ammo to switch weapons and manage your pack.

Third-party models: weapon models from *3D Retro Styled Cold War Guns*, creature models from *PSX Sea Horror Monsters*, The Wrapper, the elk demon (including the animated creature model supplied by the project owner), the PS1 barrel and PSX ammo box models, melee weapons from *POLY Survival Melee Weapons*, and The Heaving, The Writhing (classic figure), the worms and The Latching from *Cordatix PSX Horror Swarms* (cordatix.itch.io) are licensed for use inside this game only. Do not extract or redistribute them as an asset pack. The PSX cardboard boxes are CC0. First-person arms are from *PSX First Person Arms* (free game assets), with the forearms repainted as sleeves. Recorded audio is CC0: drones from *Ambience SFX Free* by foodforfaeries, hits and drops from *SFX Kit* by Wyzowl.

Controls (keyboard): WASD move, mouse aim, click fire, R reload, V kick, H medkit, G doors / breakers / chargers, B barricade, E weapons.
