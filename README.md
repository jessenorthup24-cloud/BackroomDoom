# NOCLIPPED

Bodycam backrooms survival horror shooter for mobile browsers.

**Play:** open the GitHub Pages link for this repo on your phone, then use *Add to Home Screen* (Safari) or *Install app* (Chrome) to install it. It plays fullscreen in landscape and works offline after the first load.

Controls (touch): swipe left side to move, right side to aim; it fires automatically when the crosshair is on a target. Double-tap the right side to aim down sights. Tap your health to use a medkit, tap your ammo to switch weapons and manage your pack.

Controls (keyboard): WASD move, mouse aim, click fire, R reload, V kick, H medkit, G doors / breakers / chargers, B barricade, E weapons.
