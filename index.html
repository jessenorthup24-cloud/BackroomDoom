HOW TO SURVIVE

Five chapters, three floors each. Every floor hides a KEYCARD and a red EXIT. The third floor of each chapter has a boss holding the card. Between floors pick a mutation and choose which door to take. Death ends the run, but scrap from kills and crates is banked automatically for the Breakroom.

TOUCHLeft side: move stick · Right side: drag to look · Swipe the left side to move (push far to sprint) · swipe the right side to aim · your weapon fires automatically when the dot is on a threat or crate · double-tap the right side for sights · tap the ammo count for weapons, reload, flashlight and map · ⇄ swaps weapon · ◐ flashlight
KEYBOARDWASD move · Mouse look · Click fire · Right-click sights · Shift sprint · R reload · V kick · F flashlight · G use (doors, chargers, breakers, lockers) · B barricade · H medkit · T grenade · 1–0 / wheel / Q weapons · E pack · Tab map · F1 key list · Esc pause
MEDKITSCarry up to 3. Tap your healthPress H to apply one — you can't move or shoot for 3 seconds. Vests absorb damage.
NOISEEnemies hear you. Sprinting, splashing and gunfire bring them to look. Walk and they lose track of you.
DOORSClose office doors to break line of sight. Barricade them to buy time — you'll hear them being battered.
PACKSix slots for medkits and loose ammo. Tap your ammoPress E to see the pack and drop things.
BREAKERSSome floors have no power. Flip every breaker to bring the exit online.
THE OCCUPANTStay on a floor too long and something that cannot be killed starts hunting you. Hit the glowing sac in its chest to make it stagger, hold your light on it to slow it, and get out.
BLEEDINGHeavy hits make you bleed until a medkit closes the wound.
LIGHTYour flashlight lasts about a minute and a half. Tap the battery to switch it off and save it.
LOCKERSSupply lockers hold good loot, but prying them open is loud.
CHARGE STATIONSYour flashlight slowly drains. Stand at a green wall station to recharge it — each one only holds so much.
MAGAZINESAmmo you find is loose. Reloading swaps magazines; when none are loaded you thumb rounds in one at a time. Spare mags refill by hand when it's quiet.
LAUNCHERSThe RPG-7 and Carl Gustaf fire one rocket at a time. Don't fire them at your feet. Frag grenades bounce, then blow after a short fuse.
COMBOSChain kills fast for score multipliers. Headshots deal heavy damage.
KICKAnything that gets right up on you gets kicked back automatically. It staggers them and breaks their attack.
CRATESSmash them for supplies. Red barrels explode.
TAPESThe survey team that came before you left recordings. Find them all.
SKITTERSSmall and fast. They peek around corners, spit, and dart back out of sight.
HANGERSCrawl across the ceiling and drop on you. Look up.
MANNEQUINSOnly move when you aren't looking at them.
